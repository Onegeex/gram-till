How it works

01 · SAY ITTap, speak, pause

"Charge Maria twelve." Works in your language: Spanish, Portuguese, French, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese and more.

02 · CONFIRMOne tap sends the request

The customer gets "Pay 12 GRAM" in Telegram and pays from Gram Wallet in one tap. No card machine, no currency exchange.

03 · GLANCEPAID appears in your lens

Matched to the order automatically. The customer gets a receipt, and short or unknown payments are flagged honestly.

Safe by design

No custody, no keys

Gram Till never holds money or private keys and never signs a transaction. It only watches your wallet and creates payment links.

Nothing sends without a tap

Every voice command becomes a confirm card that expires in 20 seconds. Amounts in dollars or euros are refused, not guessed.

Works anywhere

Any country and any time zone. Customers pay from any TON wallet, Gram Wallet inside Telegram included.

Set up in two minutes

  1. Open the bot (link appears here once the bot is live) and send /start: your shop name, then your wallet address.
  2. Optional: /menu latte 4, /timezone Europe/Berlin, /language es.
  3. Send /pair, open Gram Till in the Even app (or load /glasses/), and enter the code.
  4. Share /link, your counter QR, with customers. That's it.
